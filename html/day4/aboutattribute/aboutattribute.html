Attribute (noun) has several related meanings depending on the context: A quality or characteristic of someone or something (most common) An attribute is a feature, trait, or property. Examples: Patience is one of her best attributes. Speed and reliability are important attributes of a good computer. A property of an object in computing or programming An attribute is a piece of information associated with an object, element, or file. Example: In HTML, href is an attribute of the (anchor) tag. In object-oriented programming, an object's attributes might include name, color, or size. A symbolic object associated with a person or figure (especially in art or religion) An attribute helps identify a person or deity. Example: A lightning bolt is an attribute of Zeus in Greek mythology. As a verb ("to attribute"), it means to regard something as being caused by or belonging to someone or something. Example: "She attributed her success to hard work." Synonyms (noun): Characteristic Trait Feature Property Quality Example sentence: Honesty is an important attribute of a good leader.
